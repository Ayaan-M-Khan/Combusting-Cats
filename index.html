HOW TO PLAY (RULES)

EXPLODING KITTENS: If you draw an Exploding Kitten, you must play a Defuse card immediately. If you have none, you explode and lose the game!

DEFUSE: Cancels an explosion! You get to secretly insert the Exploding Kitten anywhere you want back into the Draw Deck.

ATTACK / SLAP: End your turn without drawing a card, and force the next victim to take 2 turns in a row.

SKIP: Immediately end your turn without drawing a card.

SEE THE FUTURE (3X): Privately peek at the top 3 cards of the Draw Pile.

ALTER THE FUTURE: Peek at the top 3 cards and rearrange their order!

FAVOR: Force any other player to give you 1 card of their choice.

2-OF-A-KIND (PAIRS): Play two identical matching cards to steal 1 random card from any opponent!

3-OF-A-KIND (TRIO): Play three identical matching cards to name an opponent and demand a specific card (e.g. Defuse)! If they have it, they must surrender it.

5-DIFFERENT CARDS: Play five completely different cards to scavenge the Discard Pile and claim any card of your choice!

AUTO-SORT: Toggle automatic inventory sorting anytime using the Auto-Sort button on the bottom bar.
